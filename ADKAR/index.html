    A Useful Distinction

    Training is only part of change.

    Training typically supports Knowledge and Ability. People also need context, motivation and reinforcement to sustain new behaviors.

    Training builds knowledge and provides opportunities to practise.

    Change requires more than training.

    Communication, leadership, involvement and follow-through support adoption.
